# Contributing to CVIL

## Adding a new specialization track

1. **Open an issue first** describing the specialization you want to add (e.g. *3D Vision*, *Video Understanding*, *Generative Models*, *Medical Imaging*, *Pose Estimation*, etc.), so it can be discussed before you put in the work.
2. Once it's greenlit, submit a PR that:
   - Follows the same structure as the existing tracks: a short "what is this?" framing, then grouped sub-lists (concepts → architectures/models → metrics/tricks, as relevant).
   - Stays at **checklist depth**: topic names and short framing phrases, not full explanations. The goal is to point at what to study, not to teach it inline.
   - Slots in under **Specialization Tracks** in computer-vision-interview-list.md, replacing or sitting alongside the `_Your Track Here_` placeholder.
   - Does not include a dedicated "Datasets" subsection — keep the focus on concepts, architectures, tricks, and metrics rather than naming specific datasets.
3. Keep tone and formatting consistent with the rest of the doc (heading levels, bullet style, no checkboxes).

## Other contributions

- Spotted an outdated reference, a missing must-know model, or a topic that's drifted out of relevance? Open an issue or PR.
- Disagree with how something is categorized (Core vs. Specialization)? Open an issue, happy to discuss.
